🐳 Budget Bubble – Delete your account

This page is for the Budget Bubble app (developer: Budget Bubble). You only have an online account if you signed in under Settings → Sync. The app works without one.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Budget Bubble (the phone app or the website).
  2. Go to Settings → Sync.
  3. Tap 🗑️ Delete account.
  4. Confirm, and type your password when asked.

Can't open the app, or forgot your password? Open an issue at github.com/karame-dotcom/budget-bubble-web/issues with the email you signed up with, and we will delete the account for you.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email address and password).
  • The synced copy of your books and recent chat messages stored online.

What is kept

  • The books saved on your own phone or browser are not removed. They are yours; delete them with Settings → Backup → Reset everything, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Text and photos you sent to the AI are processed by Google Gemini to answer you; we do not store them in your account. See the privacy policy.
  • Nothing else is kept. There is no additional retention period.
