🐳 Budget Bubble – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Budget Bubble is a personal bookkeeping app. Your money records belong to you. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information.

1. Your records stay on your device

Your transactions, accounts, budgets, goals, loans and game progress are saved on your phone (or in your browser for the website version). We do not run a server that reads them, and we do not sell or share them.

2. Optional: sync between phone and website

If you choose to sign in (email and password) in Settings → Sync, an encrypted-in-transit copy of your books is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) under your own account, so your phone and website stay in sync. Only you can read it. Without signing in, nothing is uploaded.

3. AI helper (Google Gemini)

When you chat with Bubbles, the text you type, and any photo, receipt or spreadsheet you choose to send, is sent to Google Gemini so it can understand and answer. Recent chat messages and summary totals may be included for context. This happens through the app's Google Firebase project, or directly with your own Gemini key if you added one in Settings. Offline "quick mode" entries are not sent anywhere. Please do not send information you do not want processed by Google; see Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Permissions the app may ask for

  • Notifications – reminders for bills, dues and daily receipts. These are created on your device.
  • Camera / photos – only when you tap to add a receipt or picture.
  • Files – to save and restore backups and reports you create (Documents/BudgetBubble) and to share them.

5. What we do not do

  • No ads and no advertising trackers.
  • No selling or renting of your data.
  • We do not access your bank accounts. You type in or import your own records.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

You can export a backup, or erase everything, any time in Settings → Backup. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on the phone. To delete the synced copy, use Start over in Settings → Backup, or contact us below and we will delete your account data.

7. Children

Budget Bubble is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/karame-dotcom/budget-bubble-web/issues
